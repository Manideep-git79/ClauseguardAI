SECTION I - PROPERTY COVERAGES
COVERAGE C - PERSONAL PROPERTY SCOPE
We cover personal property owned or used by an insured while it is anywhere in the world. At your request, we will cover personal property owned by others while on the part of the residence premises occupied by an insured, or owned by a guest or residence employee while in any residence occupied by you. Our limit of liability for personal property usually located at an insured's residence other than the primary residence premises is 10% of the limit of liability for Coverage C, or $1,000, whichever is greater.

COVERAGE D - LOSS OF USE AND ADDITIONAL LIVING EXPENSE
The limit of liability for Coverage D is the total limit for all additional living expense and fair rental value necessary to maintain your normal standard of living if a loss covered under this Section makes the residence premises not fit to live in. Payment will be for the shortest time required to repair or replace the damage or, if you permanently relocate, the shortest time required for your household to settle elsewhere, not to exceed twenty-four (24) consecutive months.

SECTION I - PERILS INSURED AGAINST
We insure for direct physical loss to the property described in Coverage C caused by the following broad perils: fire or lightning, windstorm or hail, explosion, riot or civil commotion, aircraft, vehicles, smoke, vandalism or malicious mischief, theft or attempted theft, falling objects, weight of ice, snow, or sleet, and sudden and accidental tearing apart, cracking, burning, or bulging of a steam or hot water heating system.

SECTION I - GENERAL EXCLUSIONS
We do not insure for loss caused directly or indirectly by any of the following excluded causes, regardless of any other cause or event contributing concurrently or in any sequence to the loss:
1. Flood and Water Damage, meaning flood, surface water, waves, tidal water, overflow of any body of water, storm surge, or water backing up from sewers, drains, or sump pump systems.
2. Earth Movement, including earthquake, landslide, mudflow, mine subsidence, sinkhole collapse, or earth sinking.
3. War, military action, civil insurrection, rebellion, or nuclear radiation hazard.
4. Intentional Loss or Fraud, meaning any loss arising out of any act an insured commits or conspires to commit with the intent to cause a loss or defraud the insurer.

SECTION I - DEDUCTIBLE AND LOSS RETENTION
Unless a higher deductible is specifically scheduled in the Declarations, our standard per-occurrence deductible is $500.00. We will pay only that part of the total of all loss payable under Section I that exceeds the deductible amount shown in the Declarations. No deductible shall apply to payments made under Coverage D - Additional Living Expense.

SECTION I - DUTIES AFTER LOSS AND CLAIMS PROCEDURES
In case of a loss to covered personal property, you must see that the following conditions are complied with:
1. Give prompt written notice to us or our insurance claims agent within thirty (30) days of the date of loss.
2. Notify the municipal police department immediately in case of loss resulting from theft, burglary, or vandalism.
3. Protect the property from further damage and prepare an itemized inventory of damaged property detailing the make, model, purchase date, replacement value, and amount of claim.
4. Send to us, within sixty (60) days after our request, your signed, sworn proof of loss setting forth the time and cause of loss, your interest in the property, and all other insurance policies covering the property.

SECTION I AND II - CANCELLATION AND NON-RENEWAL PROVISIONS
You may cancel this policy at any time by returning it to us or by notifying us in writing of the date cancellation is to take effect. We may cancel this policy only for the statutory reasons stated below by delivering written notice to you at your last known address:
1. Non-payment of premium: We may cancel at any time by providing at least ten (10) days advance written notice before the cancellation takes effect.
2. When this policy has been in effect for less than sixty (60) days and is not a renewal, we may cancel for any lawful underwriting reason by giving at least thirty (30) days advance written notice.
3. If this policy has been in effect for sixty (60) days or more, we may cancel only for material misrepresentation or substantial change in the risk upon giving at least thirty (30) days advance written notice.

SECTION I AND II - SUBROGATION AND RECOVERY RIGHTS
An insured may waive in writing before a loss all rights of recovery against any third party. If not waived, we may require an assignment of rights of recovery for a loss to the extent that payment is made by us. If an assignment is sought, an insured must sign and deliver all necessary instruments, preserve all evidence, and cooperate fully with us in prosecuting any legal subrogation action.

SECTION I AND II - GRACE PERIOD FOR PREMIUM PAYMENT
A grace period of thirty (30) days will be granted for the payment of each renewal premium or monthly installment falling due after the initial policy deposit, during which grace period the policy shall remain in full force and effect, subject to deduction of any unpaid premium balance from any covered loss claim.

SECTION I AND II - DISPUTE RESOLUTION AND APPRAISAL
If you and we fail to agree on the amount of loss, either party may demand an appraisal of the loss in writing. Within twenty (20) days of receiving such written demand, each party shall select an independent, qualified appraiser. The two appraisers shall choose an umpire. If they fail to agree on an umpire within fifteen (15) days, either may request that a judge of a court of record in the county where the residence premises is located select such umpire. A written award agreed to by any two will set the amount of loss. No legal action or lawsuit can be brought against us unless there has been full compliance with all policy terms and the action is started within two (2) years after the date of loss.
