RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT
SAN FRANCISCO STANDARD MULTI-FAMILY HOUSING

PARTIES AND PROPERTY IDENTIFICATION
This Residential Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is executed on October 2, 2026, by and between Landlord, Robert M. Sterling ("Landlord"), managing agent for Bayview Urban Properties LLC, with primary offices at 742 Montgomery Street, Suite 1200, San Francisco, CA 94111, phone (415) 890-4421, email rsterling.properties@baymgmt.com, and Tenant, Emily Chen ("Tenant"), phone (510) 332-9184, email emily.chen.design@gmail.com, Social Security Number 554-21-8930. Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the residential dwelling unit designated as Apartment 204, located at 1482 Valencia Street, San Francisco, CA 94110 (the "Premises").

SECTION 1: TERM AND RENT PAYMENT TERMS
The term of this lease shall commence on November 1, 2026, and continue through October 31, 2027. Monthly rent shall be payable in advance on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month in the lawful amount of $3,250.00. Rent payments must be made electronically via the tenant resident portal or by certified cashier check payable to Bayview Urban Properties LLC delivered to the management office. Rent must be paid in full without offset, deduction, or abatement.

SECTION 2: LATE FEES AND DELINQUENCY PENALTIES
If monthly rent is not received by Landlord within five (5) calendar days following the due date, Tenant shall incur an immediate non-refundable late charge of $100.00. In addition, an administrative late assessment fee of $25.00 shall accumulate for each subsequent calendar day until all past-due rent and accrued charges are paid in full. Any dishonored electronic payment or bounced check shall incur a return fee of $50.00.

SECTION 3: SECURITY DEPOSIT AND LAWFUL DEDUCTIONS
Upon execution of this Agreement, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $3,250.00 as a Security Deposit. The deposit shall be maintained in an interest-bearing escrow account at First Republic Bank of San Francisco. Within twenty-one (21) days after Tenant vacates and surrenders the Premises, Landlord shall furnish Tenant with an itemized written statement detailing any lawful deductions for unpaid rent, cleaning costs, or repair of damages beyond normal wear and tear, accompanied by a refund of any remaining deposit balance.

SECTION 4: MAINTENANCE OBLIGATIONS AND TENANT REPAIRS
Landlord shall maintain the structural components, building roof, shared hallways, foundational plumbing, central heating systems, and electrical circuits in full compliance with municipal habitability standards. Tenant shall keep the interior of the dwelling clean, sanitary, and well-ventilated, and shall promptly notify management in writing of any water leak, electrical fault, or sanitary defect. Tenant shall be financially responsible for plumbing stoppages caused by improper waste disposal.

SECTION 5: LANDLORD RIGHT OF ENTRY AND INSPECTION ACCESS
Landlord and authorized building contractors may enter the Premises during customary business hours (Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM) for the purpose of making necessary repairs, inspecting conditions, testing smoke detectors, or exhibiting the unit to prospective tenants or purchasers. Landlord shall provide reasonable advance written notice of at least twenty-four (24) hours prior to entry. In cases of immediate emergency involving fire, gas leaks, or flooding, immediate entry is permitted without notice.

SECTION 6: SUBLETTING, ASSIGNMENT, AND GUEST OCCUPANCY
Tenant shall not assign this lease, sublet any room or portion of the dwelling, or permit the dwelling to be occupied by short-term guests or vacation rental services (including Airbnb or VRBO) without obtaining the prior written consent of Landlord. Any unauthorized subletting or license to occupy shall constitute an incurable, substantial breach of tenancy and grounds for immediate eviction. Overnight guests staying longer than fourteen (14) consecutive calendar days shall require written landlord authorization.

SECTION 7: PET POLICY AND ANIMAL RESTRICTIONS
No domesticated animals, dogs, cats, reptiles, or pets of any description shall be kept, harbored, or boarded on the Premises without Landlord's prior written Pet Addendum and payment of a refundable pet deposit of $500.00. Certified service animals and medically prescribed assistance animals under the Americans with Disabilities Act and California Fair Employment and Housing Act are welcome without deposit or surcharge.

SECTION 8: UTILITIES AND MUNICIPAL CHARGES
Tenant shall register and pay directly for all electricity and natural gas service through Pacific Gas and Electric (PG&E), as well as internet, telephone, and streaming entertainment services. Landlord shall provide and pay for cold water, municipal wastewater sewer service, and scheduled curbside trash collection provided by Recology San Francisco.

SECTION 9: RENTER LIABILITY AND PROPERTY INSURANCE
Tenant is strictly required to obtain and maintain an active policy of renter's insurance throughout the entire duration of the tenancy, with personal liability coverage of not less than $300,000.00 and personal contents property coverage adequate for Tenant's possessions. Tenant must provide a Certificate of Insurance naming Bayview Urban Properties LLC as an interested party within ten (10) calendar days of taking physical occupancy.

SECTION 10: INDEMNIFICATION AND HOLD HARMLESS
Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Landlord, its agents, and employees against any and all claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and reasonable attorney fees arising out of personal injury or property damage caused directly by Tenant, Tenant's family, licensees, invitees, or pets on the Premises or in common areas of the property.

SECTION 11: TERMINATION AND NOTICE TO VACATE
Either party may terminate this tenancy upon the conclusion of the initial lease term by providing written notice of termination to the other party at least thirty (30) days prior to the proposed surrender date. If Tenant vacates the Premises prior to the expiration of the term without lawful statutory cause, Tenant shall remain fully liable for monthly rent until the unit is re-leased to a qualified replacement tenant.

SECTION 12: AUTOMATIC RENEWAL AND EXTENSION
Upon expiration of the initial one-year term, this Agreement shall automatically convert into a month-to-month tenancy upon the identical terms and conditions herein, unless either Landlord or Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date. Monthly rent upon conversion may be adjusted subject to applicable San Francisco Rent Ordinance guidelines.

SECTION 13: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Agreement shall be construed, interpreted, and governed under the laws of the State of California and the municipal ordinances of the City and County of San Francisco. Any judicial action or legal proceeding relating to this tenancy shall be initiated and maintained in the Superior Court of California for the County of San Francisco.
